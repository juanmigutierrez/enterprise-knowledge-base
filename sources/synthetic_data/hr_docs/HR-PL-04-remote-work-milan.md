# Remote work – Milan office

## The rule
Employees based in the Milan office may work remotely up to **2 days per week**. Tuesday and Thursday are office days for everyone, so teams meet in person.

## Working from abroad
You may work from another EU country for up to **20 working days per year**. Tell your manager and the People team at least one week before you go.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset. Employees who work remotely receive a home-office allowance of **€25 per month**, paid with the salary.

## Exceptions
Longer remote periods (for health, caring duties or after parental leave, see HR-PL-03) are agreed with your manager and the People team and reviewed every three months.
