# Annual leave policy

## Who this applies to
Every employee, in every country. Local rules (see HR-PL-02 for Italy) add to this policy; where they give you more, the local rule wins.

## How much leave you get
- Full-time employees get **25 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Part-time employees get the same number of days, reduced in proportion to their contracted hours.
- Leave builds up monthly: about 2.08 days for each full month worked.

## Booking leave
1. Request leave in the HR portal at least **two weeks** before the first day off.
2. Your manager approves or declines within three working days.
3. Requests of more than ten consecutive working days need approval one month in advance.

## Carrying leave over
You may carry up to **5 unused days** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**; after that they are lost unless local law says otherwise.
