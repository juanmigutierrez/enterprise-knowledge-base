# Business travel policy (2024, superseded)

Superseded by HR-PL-06 from 1 January 2026. Kept only until the archive clean-up.

## Transport
- Flights are allowed for any journey over **2 hours**.
- Business class is allowed for flights over **6 hours**.

## Hotels and meals
- Hotel limit per night: **€120** in all cities.
- Meal allowance: **€40 per day**.

## Claiming expenses
Submit receipts within **60 days**.
