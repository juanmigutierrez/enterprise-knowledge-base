# Expenses and business travel policy (2026)

This policy replaces the 2024 travel policy (HR-PL-99) from 1 January 2026.

## Before you travel
- Book trips through the travel portal; bookings made elsewhere are not refunded.
- Trips abroad need your manager's approval in the portal before booking.

## Transport
- Trains are the default for journeys under **4 hours**; flights only above that.
- Economy class for all flights. Premium economy is allowed for flights over **8 hours**.

## Hotels and meals
- Hotel limit per night: **€150** in most cities, **€220** in London, Paris and Toronto.
- Meal allowance: up to **€50 per day** when travelling, with receipts.

## Claiming expenses
Submit receipts in the expenses tool within **30 days** of the expense. Claims older than 90 days are not refunded.
