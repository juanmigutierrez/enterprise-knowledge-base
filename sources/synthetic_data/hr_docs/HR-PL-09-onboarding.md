# Onboarding – your first two weeks

## Before day one
- [ ] Sign your contract and return the personal data form.
- [ ] Your manager orders your laptop and access badge.

## Day one
- [ ] Collect your laptop from IT and set up two-factor sign-in.
- [ ] Meet your onboarding buddy.
- [ ] Read the code of conduct (HR-PL-10) and confirm it in the HR portal.

## First week
- [ ] Complete the security and data-protection training (about 45 minutes).
- [ ] Book your first annual leave days so you know how the HR portal works (HR-PL-01).

## Second week
- [ ] Agree your 90-day goals with your manager.
- [ ] Give feedback on your onboarding in the survey the People team sends you.
