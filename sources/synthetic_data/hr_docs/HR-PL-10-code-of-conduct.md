# Code of conduct

## Respect
We treat colleagues, retailers, brands and shoppers with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.

## Conflicts of interest
Tell your manager if you, a relative or a close friend has a financial interest in a retailer, brand or supplier you work with. Gifts above **€75** in value must be declared in the HR portal.

## Data and confidentiality
Shopper data, retailer contracts and campaign results are confidential. Share them only with people who need them for their work, and only through company tools.

## Raising a concern
Speak to your manager, the People team, or the confidential whistleblowing channel. Reports can be anonymous, and nobody may be penalised for raising a concern in good faith.
